Our work

What we do for practices, line by line

We do not publish client case studies. This is the work itself: nine service lines, what each one includes, and how to get started.

Service lines

Nine things we run for a practice

Every line below is a standing piece of work we take over from the front desk and the back office. The deliverables are the same ones listed on the services page.

  1. 01

    Medical billing & scrubbing

    Charges entered, coded and scrubbed for errors before submission, so claims go out right the first time and payment is not delayed by rework.

    • Claims scrubbed before submission
    • Daily electronic claims
    • 24-hour denial appeals
  2. 02

    Accounts receivable recovery

    We follow up on unpaid claims at 30, 60 and 90+ days, resolve payer rejections and keep aging balances from turning into write-offs.

    • Aged claims worked on a schedule
    • Aged 90+ day claims audit
    • Dedicated appeals specialist
  3. 03

    Insurance verification

    Real-time pre-appointment patient eligibility and benefit verification, preventing denied claims due to terminated coverage or copay errors.

    • 24-hour advance verification
    • Copay & deductible tracking
    • Prior auth confirmation
  4. 04

    Provider credentialing

    Our team handles Medicare, Medicaid and private commercial payer enrollment, so physicians meet the standards to bill in-network.

    • Fast-track payer enrollment
    • CAQH & NPI maintenance
    • Contract fee schedule audit
  5. 05

    Re-validation

    Timely payer re-validation documentation and credential maintenance to avoid network dropouts and hold-backs.

    • Medicare PECOS sync
    • Payment hold-back prevention
    • Expiration alert tracking
  6. 06

    EFT & ERA setup

    Direct electronic funds transfer and electronic remittance advice setup for direct bank deposit payments without paper check delays.

    • Automated auto-posting
    • Direct bank deposit
    • 835 ERA reconciliation
  7. 07

    MIPS reporting

    MIPS reporting for eligible clinicians: quality measure selection, score tracking and the CMS payment adjustments that depend on them.

    • Quality measure selection
    • Positive payment adjustments
    • Penalty avoidance planning
  8. 08

    CCM & RPM programs

    Turn-key Chronic Care Management and Remote Patient Monitoring workflows that add monthly recurring practice income.

    • CPT 99490 & 99453 billing
    • Recurring monthly revenue
    • 20-minute clinical logging
  9. 09

    Telehealth billing

    Virtual care coding (CPT 99421-99423, GT modifiers) so digital health encounters are reimbursed in full.

    • GT/95 modifier scrubbing
    • E-visit & tele-consult claims
    • Full payer parity support
