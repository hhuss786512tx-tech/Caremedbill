Psychiatry & Behavioral Health Medical Billing Services

Psychiatry and behavioral health billing services covering time-based coding, documentation, and denial prevention.

Book a free Psychiatry & Behavioral Health billing audit

Psychiatry and behavioral health billing depends heavily on time-based coding and documentation that clearly supports medical necessity, requirements that differ meaningfully from most other specialties. Denials are common when session length, modality, or diagnosis documentation isn't airtight.

Common Psychiatry & Behavioral Health Billing Challenges

  • Accurate time-based coding for therapy and medication management visits
  • Documentation requirements that clearly support medical necessity
  • Denials tied to incomplete session or modality documentation
  • Coordinating billing across telehealth and in-person visits

How Care Med Billing Helps Psychiatry & Behavioral Health Practices

  • Coders experienced with time-based behavioral health coding
  • Documentation guidance to reduce medical-necessity denials
  • Telehealth billing support alongside in-person visit billing
  • Ongoing claim scrubbing and A/R follow-up
