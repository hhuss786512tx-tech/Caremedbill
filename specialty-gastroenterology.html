Specialty Medical Billing

Gastroenterology Medical Billing Services

Gastroenterology billing services for colonoscopy, endoscopy, office-based GI services, and prior authorization management.

Book Free Gastroenterology Billing Audit

Gastroenterology practices bill across a wide mix of office visits, endoscopic procedures, and diagnostic testing — each with its own coding rules, modifier requirements, and prior authorization hurdles. Screening versus diagnostic colonoscopy coding alone is a common source of denials and patient billing complaints when it isn't handled carefully.

Common Gastroenterology Billing Challenges

  • Distinguishing screening vs. diagnostic colonoscopy coding correctly
  • Modifier use on endoscopy and office-based procedures performed same-day
  • Prior authorization delays affecting scheduling and revenue timing
  • Denials tied to incomplete procedure documentation
  • Coordinating billing between office visits and ambulatory surgical centers

How Care Med Billing Helps Gastroenterology Practices

  • Coders trained specifically on GI procedural and E/M coding
  • Dedicated prior-authorization follow-up to reduce scheduling delays
  • Pre-submission claim scrubbing built around common GI denial reasons
  • Ongoing A/R follow-up on aged endoscopy and procedure claims
