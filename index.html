Our Services

Dedicated Nationwide Billing Services

Care Medical Billing & Management Services is specialized in serving comprehensive billing solutions tailored across the US with over 15 years of industry experience.

Medical Billing

Our seamless medical billing process enhances efficiency by minimizing errors, enabling accurate revenue management to increase cash flow.

Account Receivable Recovery

We help you boost and increase the efficiency of your cash flow by minimizing claim denials and recovering outdated AR.

Insurance Verification & Credentialing

Verifying physicians meet payer standards, navigating enrollment attestations, and streamlining credentialing timelines.

Re-Validation & EFT Setup

Reviewing re-validation documents electronically while setting up dependable electronic funds transfers (EFT) to provider accounts.

Merit-Based Incentive Payment System (MIPS)

Customized data-driven reporting service to help healthcare providers navigate MIPS reporting, compliance, and incentives.

CCM, RPM & PCM Solutions

Chronic Care Management (CCM), Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), and Principal Care Management (PCM) clinical billing workflows.
