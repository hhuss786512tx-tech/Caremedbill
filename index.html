Comprehensive Healthcare Billing & RCM Solutions

Care Medical Billing & Management Services delivers specialized, transparent, and accurate billing infrastructure tailored to your clinical practice across the US.

Medical Billing

Our seamless medical billing process enhances efficiency by minimizing coding errors, enabling accurate revenue management to increase cash flow.

Accounts Receivable Recovery

We boost the efficiency of your cash flow by minimizing claim denials, recovering outdated AR, and validating patient benefits to ensure steady practice growth.

Insurance Verification & Credentialing

Assisting healthcare providers to navigate enrollment attestations, verifying physicians meet payer standards, and streamlining credentialing timelines.

Re-Validation & EFT Setup

Ensuring efficient re-validation by reviewing information, uploading supporting documents, and setting up electronic funds transfers (EFT) to provider accounts.

Merit-Based Incentive Payment System (MIPS)

Customized service to help healthcare providers navigate the complexities of MIPS reporting, compliance, and enabling incentives with expert data-driven strategies.

CCM, RPM & PCM Clinical Workflows

Chronic Care Management (CCM), Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), and Principal Care Management (PCM) specialized workflows to generate recurring clinical revenue.
