Everything between the visit and the payment.

From eligibility checks before the appointment to the follow-up calls after the claim goes out, we run the whole revenue cycle, or just the part your practice needs.

  1. 01

    Medical billing

    Charge entry, coding review, claim scrubbing and submission to commercial and government payers, with denials worked until they are paid.

  2. 02

    Accounts receivable recovery

    We go after unpaid and aging claims, including old AR that has been sitting for months, and report back on what comes in.

  3. 03

    Insurance verification

    Eligibility and benefits confirmed before the visit, so coverage problems and copay surprises are caught at the front desk, not after the claim.

  4. 04

    Credentialing

    Enrollment with Medicare, Medicaid and commercial payers, CAQH upkeep and NPI registration. Payers typically take 60 to 120 days; we keep the file moving.

  5. 05

    Re-validation

    Payer re-validation paperwork reviewed, supporting documents uploaded, signed and submitted on time so you do not drop out of network.

  6. 06

    EFT and ERA setup

    Electronic payments straight to your account and electronic remittance posted against claims. No paper checks waiting on a desk.

  7. 07

    MIPS reporting

    Measure selection, data collection and submission for the Merit-based Incentive Payment System, so reporting does not turn into a penalty.

  8. 08

    Reports and follow-ups

    Customizable monthly and quarterly financial reports, plus the payer follow-up calls nobody on your staff has time to make.

  9. 09

    CCM, RPM and PCM programs

    Chronic Care Management, Remote Patient Monitoring and Principal Care Management for your Medicare patients, run and billed by us.

  10. 10

    Telehealth, charge entry, appeals and more

    Telehealth billing, transcription, structured charge entry, payment posting, appeals and web-based billing software round out the list.

How an account runs, month to month.

No surprises. The same five steps for every practice, with a named team you can call.

  1. Verify

    Eligibility and benefits are checked before the patient is seen, so the claim starts clean.

  2. Code and enter

    Charges are captured and double-checked for completeness before anything goes out.

  3. Scrub and submit

    Clean claims are submitted within 24 hours of receiving the charges.

  4. Follow up

    Denials are appealed, payers are called, and aging AR is worked on a schedule, not when someone remembers.

  5. Report

    Monthly and quarterly financial reports, and claim status you can check any time.

Questions we hear first.

If yours is not here, call +1 888 865 5485 or send a note. We reply during business hours.

What types of healthcare providers can benefit from your billing services?
Our services are scalable and versatile, so they can be tailored to support a wide range of providers, including independent practitioners, clinics, and hospital settings.
How can RCM benefit my healthcare facility?
RCM can reduce administrative burden, increase the rate of clean claims, and improve cash flow, leading to fewer claim rejections and better revenue collection for your practice.
How long does the credentialing process take?
Credentialing can take between 60 and 120 days depending on the insurance companies and the completeness of your documents. Our dedicated team works to streamline the process, reducing delays and keeping approvals on time.
What services are included in your RCM solutions?
Our RCM solutions include scheduling, insurance verification, coding, claims submission, denial management, and collections, all tailored to your practice's revenue and return on investment.
Do you handle both insurance and patient billing?
Yes. We provide a comprehensive solution covering both insurance claims and patient billing so your practice collects what it is owed.
Why is credentialing important for my practice?
Credentialing is essential to make sure providers are eligible to bill insurance companies, and it helps maintain compliance with federal and state regulations, which protects your revenue stream.
