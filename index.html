Our Core Expertise

Dedicated Nationwide Billing & RCM Solutions

Care Medical Billing & Management Services is specialized in serving comprehensive billing solutions tailored across the US. Our obligation is to be transparent, accurate, and efficient.

Medical Billing

Our seamless medical billing process enhances efficiency by minimizing errors, enabling accurate and effective revenue management to increase cash flow.

Accounts Receivable Recovery

We boost the efficiency of your cash flow by minimizing claim denials, recovering outdated AR, and validating patient needs to keep your practice financially healthy.

Insurance Verification & Credentialing

Assisting healthcare providers to navigate attestation of enrollment, verifying physicians meet payer standards, and streamlining 60-120 day credentialing timelines.

Re-Validation & EFT Setup

Ensuring efficient re-validation by reviewing information, uploading supporting documents, and setting up dependable electronic funds transfers (EFT) to provider accounts.

Merit-Based Incentive Payment System (MIPS)

Customized service to help healthcare providers navigate the complexities of MIPS reporting, compliance, and enabling incentives with expert data-driven strategies.

CCM, RPM & PCM Clinical Workflows

Chronic Care Management (CCM), Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), and Principal Care Management (PCM) specialized workflows to generate recurring clinical revenue.
