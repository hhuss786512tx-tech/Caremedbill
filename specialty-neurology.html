Specialty Medical Billing

Neurology Medical Billing Services

Neurology billing services covering complex diagnostic testing, chronic condition documentation, and coding accuracy.

Book Free Neurology Billing Audit

Neurology billing combines complex diagnostic testing — EEGs, EMGs, nerve conduction studies — with long-term management of chronic neurological conditions. Documentation has to clearly support medical necessity for repeat testing, or claims are quickly flagged for review.

Common Neurology Billing Challenges

  • Correct coding for EEG, EMG, and nerve conduction studies
  • Medical necessity documentation for repeat diagnostic testing
  • Denials tied to chronic condition management documentation gaps
  • Coordinating billing across office, hospital, and diagnostic lab settings

How Care Med Billing Helps Neurology Practices

  • Coders experienced with neurology diagnostic and E/M billing
  • Pre-submission review focused on medical necessity documentation
  • CCM program support for chronic neurological condition patients
  • Ongoing A/R follow-up and denial management

RPM & CCM Opportunities for Neurology Practices

Patients managing chronic neurological conditions such as epilepsy, migraine, or MS may qualify for Chronic Care Management, adding a recurring revenue stream to a neurology practice.
