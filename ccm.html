Chronic Care Management (CCM)

CCM provides ongoing care coordination for Medicare patients with two or more chronic conditions. CareMedBill provides turn-key clinical documentation tracking to support CPT 99490 billing.

  • Average $62+ per enrolled patient/mo
  • Turn-key patient consent & care plan tracking
  • 20 minutes monthly non-face-to-face care management

Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)

RPM enables daily transmission of patient physiological vitals (blood pressure, glucose, weight) via pre-configured cellular devices without Wi-Fi requirements.

  • Average $120+ per enrolled patient/mo
  • Turn-key cellular device logistics & shipping
  • Automatic CPT 99453, 99454, 99457 claim scrubbing
