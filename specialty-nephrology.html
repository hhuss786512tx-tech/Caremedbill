Specialty Medical Billing

Nephrology Medical Billing Services

Nephrology billing services covering dialysis, chronic kidney disease management, and RPM/CCM programs.

Book Free Nephrology Billing Audit

Nephrology billing involves recurring dialysis-related claims, chronic kidney disease management, and a patient population that's especially well-suited to remote monitoring programs. Coding errors and missed monthly capitation opportunities are two of the most common ways nephrology practices leave revenue on the table.

Common Nephrology Billing Challenges

  • Correct coding for dialysis-related monthly capitation payments
  • Chronic kidney disease staging documentation requirements
  • Denials tied to incomplete or inconsistent lab-result documentation
  • Coordinating billing across dialysis centers, hospital, and office visits

How Care Med Billing Helps Nephrology Practices

  • Coders experienced with nephrology and dialysis-specific billing rules
  • Structured monthly capitation tracking and reconciliation
  • RPM and CCM program support for CKD patients
  • Ongoing denial management and A/R follow-up

RPM & CCM Opportunities for Nephrology Practices

Chronic kidney disease patients are ideal candidates for both Chronic Care Management and Remote Patient Monitoring, giving nephrology practices a strong recurring revenue opportunity alongside dialysis billing.
