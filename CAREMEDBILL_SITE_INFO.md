Faqs Frequently Asked Have Any Question?
01. What types of healthcare providers can benefit from your billing services?
Our services are scalable and versatile, meaning they can be tailored to support a wide range of providers, including independent practitioners, clinics, and hospital settings.
02. How can RCM benefit my healthcare facility?
RCM can reduce administrative burden, increase the rate of clean claims, and improve cash flow, leading to fewer claim rejections and enhanced revenue collection for your practice.
03. How long does the credentialing process take?
Credentialing can take between 60-120 days depending on the insurance companies and completeness of your documents. Our dedicated team works to streamline this process, reducing delays and ensuring timely approvals.
04. What services are included in your RCM solutions?
Our RCM solutions include scheduling, insurance verification, coding, claims submission, denial management, and collections, all of which are tailored to boost your practice’s revenue and ROI.
05. Do you handle both insurance and patient billing?
Yes, we provide a comprehensive solution covering both insurance claims and patient billing to ensure your practice maximizes its revenue potential.
06. Why is credentialing important for my practice?
Credentialing is essential to ensuring that providers are eligible to bill insurance companies, and it helps maintain compliance with federal and state regulations, ultimately optimizing your revenue stream.

### Medical Billing

Our seamless medical billing process enhances efficiency by minimizing errors enabling accurate and effective revenue management. We reduce your administrative burden and increase the cash flow.

### Account Receivable

We are here to help you boost and increase the efficiency of your cash flow by minimizing the denial of claims to keep your patients happy. We validate your patients needs, satisfaction and keep them up-to-date with the certain benefits you provide.

### Insurance Verification

We help our medical billing companies & providers by identifying their denied or unclaimed and improving over all the cash flow with an effective and streamlined approach.

### Credentialing

Our team assists healthcare providers to navigate the attestation of enrollment process, by verifying that physicians meet the necessary standards to offer services to patients in the Payer’s network.

Our holistic approach ensures a dependable financial institute for the electronic transfer of funds to the provider’s account. This process allows to perform comprehensive payment information for accuracy.

### Crucial Follow-Ups

We provide crucial follow ups on understanding the outstanding claims, and patient balances confirming no revenue lost. Our dedicated team tracks your unpaid claims to secure the payments you deserve.

### Web-Based Medical Billing Software

Our state-of-the-art, web-based medical billing software allows for seamless, real-time access to billing and payment information, enhancing your operational efficiency and providing you with the tools to manage your practice effectively. This software integrates with your existing systems, offering features such as automated claim submission, real-time reporting, and patient account management, all accessible from any location.

Our billing services extend to telehealth, ensuring accurate coding and billing for virtual visits. We stay up-to-date with the latest telehealth regulations to maximize your reimbursement. Our skilled team manages appeals for denial claims, and highlights the overturning denials and ensures the secure payments your practice deserves. We analyze the denials of claims and gather the necessary documents and submit the detailed appeals for reimbursement.

### Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)
