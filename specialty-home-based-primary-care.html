House Call & Home-Based Primary Care Medical Billing Services

House call and home-based primary care billing services, including place-of-service coding and RPM/CCM programs.

Book a free House Call & Home-Based Primary Care billing audit

Home-based primary care billing has its own place-of-service and documentation requirements that differ from a standard office visit, and practices that don't code for the home setting correctly often see reduced reimbursement. This is also one of the strongest settings for chronic care management programs, since home-based patients are frequently managing multiple chronic conditions.

Common House Call & Home-Based Primary Care Billing Challenges

  • Correct place-of-service coding for home and domiciliary visits
  • Documentation requirements specific to home-based E/M visits
  • Denials tied to travel time or visit-type documentation gaps
  • Underused RPM/CCM opportunities for a chronically complex patient base

How Care Med Billing Helps House Call & Home-Based Primary Care Practices

  • Coders experienced with home-based and place-of-service billing rules
  • RPM and CCM program setup support for chronically complex patients
  • Pre-submission claim scrubbing to reduce denials
  • Ongoing A/R follow-up and transparent reporting
