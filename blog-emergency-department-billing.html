Emergency Department Billing: Common Challenges and Best Practices

Care Med Billing team Specialty Billing

Emergency department billing has to move fast without sacrificing accuracy — a difficult balance, since ER documentation is often written under time pressure that other specialties don't face.

E/M Level Coding Accuracy

ER visit level coding depends entirely on documentation that clearly supports the complexity and risk involved. Undercoding is common when documentation understates the actual clinical decision-making that occurred during a busy shift.

High Volume Requires Consistent Process

ER claim volume is high and constant, which means even a small per-claim error rate compounds into significant revenue loss across a month. Scrubbing has to happen fast and consistently, not just when time allows.

Professional vs. Facility Billing

ER billing typically separates professional (physician) billing from facility billing, and coordination between the two — without duplicating or dropping charges — is a common operational challenge.

Best Practices

24-hour claim scrubbing turnaround, coders trained specifically on ER E/M leveling, and a documentation feedback loop back to providers are the three practices that most reliably improve ER billing accuracy.
